# Anti-AI-Design Rules & Heuristics

> **Core Principle**: The goal is not to make interfaces look unusual. The goal is to make them look intentionally designed. 
> 
> *Important*: We do not ban gradients, cards, drop shadows, or rounded corners outright. We eliminate *mindless, repetitive, template-driven defaults* and replace them with purposeful craft.

---

## 1. The AI Template Checklist (What to Identify & Reject)

When inspecting an interface or planning a build, scan for these telltale signs of generic AI generation:

1. **The Default SaaS Gradient**: Multi-stop radial gradients transitioning from royal blue (`#3b82f6`) to vivid purple/indigo (`#8b5cf6`), often floating behind a centered hero section.
2. **Endless Card Grids**: Three or four identical rounded cards side-by-side with a subtle drop shadow, a small icon in a tinted square box, a bold title, and a short paragraph.
3. **Indiscriminate Rounded Corners**: Applying `rounded-2xl` or `rounded-full` to every single element—buttons, containers, inputs, and image frames—regardless of structural hierarchy.
4. **The Clone Landing Page Structure**: Centered badge ("✨ Introducing v2.0") -> Massive bold title -> Subtitle -> Two pill buttons (Primary gradient, Secondary outline) -> Glowing mockup screenshot in a skewed perspective frame.
5. **Gratuitous Glow & Glassmorphism**: Neon blobs, glowing borders (`border-purple-500/50`), and heavy backdrop blur applied without structural or lighting justification.
6. **Emoji & Icon Overload**: Plastering generic outline icons or random emojis in every table cell, bullet point, and card header as decorative filler.
7. **Sterile Typography**: Default system sans-serif (`Inter` or `Roboto`) with uniform font weights and standard spacing, lacking editorial character or typographic scale contrast.

---

## 2. Diagnosing Template-Generated Feel

Ask yourself these diagnostic questions during review:
- *Could this layout belong to any random startup generated by an LLM generator yesterday?* If yes, the design lacks a distinct point of view.
- *Are elements placed there because they solve a user or brand need, or because "modern landing pages always have a 3-column feature grid"?*
- *Is the motion and styling reinforcing the content, or acting as decorative camouflage for weak typography and layout?*

---

## 3. Replacing Generic Patterns with Intentional Decisions

| Generic AI Pattern | Intentional Design Alternative |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Centered 3-column feature grid** | Asymmetrical editorial layout, horizontal narrative scroller, or high-contrast alternating typographic splits. |
| **Default blue-to-purple gradient background** | Deep textured canvas, rich monochromatic contrast, or a bespoke palette derived from product domain. |
| **Uniform rounded cards (`rounded-xl`)** | Deliberate rectangular containers with crisp 1px borders, subtle multi-layered shadows, or varied corner radii based on hierarchy. |
| **Pill buttons with default drop shadows** | Clean, tactile buttons with precise padding, subtle border highlights, and tactile active-state compression. |
| **Generic feature icons in tinted boxes** | High-impact typography, data visualization, or art-directed imagery instead of low-value icon decoration. |

## 4. Before vs. After Practical Examples

### Example A: Hero Section
- **❌ Before (Generic AI)**:
  - Centered text alignment.
  - Badge: "🚀 The future of AI workflow automation is here"
  - Title: "Scale your business with intelligent software" in standard gradient text.
  - Buttons: [Get Started (Purple Gradient)] [Book Demo (Outline)].
  - Background: Faint floating blur blobs on solid gray.
- **✔️ After (Intentionally Designed)**:
  - Left-aligned asymmetrical editorial layout.
  - Oversized serif headline (`Instrument Serif`, 5.5rem) with tight tracking and a single stark accent color.
  - Generous whitespace on the right framing a precision data preview or high-contrast monochrome product view.
  - Buttons with crisp tactile borders and subtle hover state scaling.

### Example B: Feature Section
- **❌ Before (Generic AI)**:
  - Three identical white cards with drop shadows, each featuring a purple icon box, bold title, and description paragraph.
- **✔️ After (Intentionally Designed)**:
  - A structured editorial list with bold numbered typographic markers (`01`, `02`, `03`) paired with generous negative space, variable weight hierarchy, and clean divider rules.

---

## 5. Guardrails & Balance

- **Do not force complexity**: A dense data dashboard or a developer utility tool should be razor-sharp, fast, and minimalist. Do not inject 3D models or artistic editorial flourishes where functional clarity is required.
- **Context is King**: Choose your aesthetic direction based on the product domain (fintech requires absolute precision and trust; creative agencies allow bold typography and spatial asymmetry).
- **Usability First**: Never sacrifice accessibility (WCAG contrast), touch targets, or responsive layout flow for the sake of "originality."
